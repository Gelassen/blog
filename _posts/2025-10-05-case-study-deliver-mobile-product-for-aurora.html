Executive summary

This week our team successfully delivered the first version of a mobile product based on Aurora OS for a leading player in the electronic corporate voting domain in Russia.

This week our team and I successfully delivered the first version of mobile product based on Aurora OS for a key player in electronic corporate voting business domain in Russia.

The product is a Aurora version of their Android and iOS mobile clients which allows investors to vote on regular coporate business meetings. Aurora OS is a relatively new player among mobile operating systems with growing market share in Russia.

Due migration we increased our expertise in the development of Aurora OS based software projects by investigating tradeoffs, pitfalls and caveats of Aurora OS.

The successfull delivery of this product opens an opportunity to migrate a whole ecosystem of applications to a new mobile OS to mitigate threats of mass surveilance caused by Western and Eastern governments and companies.
