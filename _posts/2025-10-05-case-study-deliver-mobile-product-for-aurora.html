Executive summary

This week our team successfully delivered the first version of a mobile product based on Aurora OS for a leading player in the electronic corporate voting domain in Russia.

Business Context

The product is an Aurora OS version of the company’s existing Android and iOS clients, enabling investors to participate in regular shareholder meetings and vote electronically. With Aurora OS gradually expanding its presence in the Russian mobile ecosystem, this project addresses the need for business-critical applications on a secure, locally supported platform.

Technical Insights

Migration to Aurora OS provided us with hands-on experience in adapting mobile applications to a relatively new operating system. During development we investigated technical pitfalls and caveats of Aurora OS, strengthening our expertise in C++/Qt, CMake, and RPM-based packaging workflows.

This knowledge will help us accelerate future migrations and reduce risks associated with system-specific issues.

Strategic Impact

The successful delivery of this application demonstrates that complex enterprise-grade solutions can be effectively migrated to Aurora OS. Beyond a single product, it opens the opportunity to transition an entire ecosystem of corporate applications onto a new mobile OS, ensuring technological independence and reducing exposure to mass surveillance risks posed by foreign governments and corporations.

Conclusion

This case marks a significant step forward in building secure, business-critical mobile solutions for Aurora OS. It strengthens both our client’s market position and our team’s expertise in a rapidly evolving technological landscape.
