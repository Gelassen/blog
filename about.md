    <p>
      I am an independent Systems Engineer working at the intersection of software engineering, applied research and complex systems.
    </p>

    <p>
      My work focuses on understanding how technical and organizational systems behave, why they fail, and how engineering can make them more resilient, efficient and easier to operate.
    </p>

    <p>
      Over the past 15 years I have worked across software engineering, AI, cybersecurity, data engineering, mobile development and industrial systems. While technologies have evolved, my core interest has remained the same: solving complex problems through systems thinking, practical engineering and continuous learning.
    </p>

    <p>
      I enjoy exploring unfamiliar domains, identifying recurring patterns and turning observations into practical solutions. Whether the problem involves software, manufacturing, security or organizational processes, my goal is to understand the system first and improve it through thoughtful engineering.
    </p>

    <p>
      I particularly enjoy exchanging ideas with engineers, researchers and practitioners working on challenging real-world problems.
    </p>
